# mt-md privacy policy

mt-md is a Markdown viewer for Chrome and Firefox. It collects no data.

- **No collection, no accounts, no analytics.** mt-md has no server and sends nothing about you, your documents or your browsing anywhere.
- **No network requests of its own.** All code, including the diagram and maths renderers, is bundled in the extension. Images and other resources that a document points at load from wherever they point, as they would in any web page.
- **What it stores, on your device only.** Your settings (theme, toolbar, editor choice and so on), the addresses of your last two documents (for the Back button), and the optional list of folders to render. If you turn on theme sync, your own themes are copied between your devices by your browser's built-in sync; mt-md itself sends nothing.
- **Local files.** With your browser's "Allow access to file URLs" switch on, mt-md reads the `.md` files you open, and an optional `mt-md-theme.css` in the same folder. Their contents stay in your browser.
- **Feedback.** The Feedback link opens the project's GitHub issue page, or an email or a link you choose in Settings, only when you click it. It pre-fills only the mt-md version, the browser name and the kind of page (local file, localhost or website), and nothing is sent until you submit it yourself.
- **Websites.** mt-md runs on local files and localhost, and on other sites only after you choose "Allow this site" or "Allow all sites"; you can remove them again.

Questions: feedback@modeltether.dev, or open an issue at https://github.com/modeltether/mt-md/issues.
